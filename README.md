## Hi there 📍

I'm Bailey Walker. I graduated from Middlebury College in Febuary 2025 with a joint degree in Geography and Environmental Studies. 
I am passionate about using GIS and data science to tell complex stories about our world.

I am an educator working with middle school and high school students in the classroom and continuing to teach out of the classroom with the Colorado Outward Bound School.

I work in QGIS, RStudio, Google Earth Engine, Adobe Illustrator, and Affinity Designer to compile and visualize spatial data focused on relationships between humans and our environment.

I'm working to build out my portfolio from my cartographic work after and during college. Stay tuned for repositories of cartographic design, Google Earth Engine apps and more!
